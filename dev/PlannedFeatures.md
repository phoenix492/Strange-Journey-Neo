# Planned Features

## Health & Stamina - Paragliders
- Bosses drop Heart Containers on their first kill for a player!
- The paraglider has been buffed slightly (33% increase to speed), making it an extremely attractive option for travel
- It also makes stamina vessels a more valuable reward.
- I don't know where to put stamina vessels yet. Maybe rewards for exploring directly?

## Variant Removal
- Stairs, Posts, Fences, Slabs
- ...Banisters, Gates, Hollow Logs, Carpets
- All of these have their item forms *removed*. 
- Instead, you'll place them directly using Quark's variant wheel.
- They'll also drop their block form directly. 
- All recipes that used these will be adjusted. All recipes that make them will be removed.

## Stargate Journey
- All dimensions should be behind stargate travel.
  - ...Not including technical dimensions or dimensions with very solid lore travel (Deeper Down)
- The Nether's address will be given for free. All others will be located in special dungeons there.
- Stargates are adjusted to be free BUT can only be kept open for a short time.
- They can instead be kept open permanently with LARGE amounts of FE/tick (enough that it's logistically difficult to insert)

## Gateways to Eternity, Hostile Neural Networks
- Custom "arena" dimension, "adventure" mode (no flight, no breaking blocks that were already there)
- Custom Gateways to Eternity with waves.
- Even include some boss rush gateways
- Drop Strange Matter (currency)
- Themed gateways drop Hostile Neural Network models of a set quality.

## Nether Dungeons
- Lunar Citadel: The End
  - Bedrock roof is reinstated above the nether, but Lunar Nether remains.
  - This will be a giant tower dungeon that reaches from the bottom of the nether, through the bedrock, to the outside
  - Address for the End on top as well as access to the surface
- The Hive: The Bumblezone
  - Layered structure full of bees and other insectoid enemies
  - The address is in the center of all the layers.
- The Biodome: Tropicraft
  - Lush, Forested glass dome
  - Even overrides the local biome
  - Contains liquid water in the nether
  - full of aggressive wildlife, like tigers.
